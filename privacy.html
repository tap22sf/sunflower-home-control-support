Summary

Sunflower Home Control communicates directly with a controller you configure. The app does not require an account, include advertising or analytics SDKs, track you, or send app usage or controller data to developer-operated servers.

Information on your device

The app saves your controller's hostname or IP address in local app preferences so you do not need to enter it each time. It holds controller state, schedules, and weather readings in memory while connected. It does not maintain a persistent history of commands or controller readings.

To change the saved address, disconnect and edit it in the app's Settings. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local app preferences. Apple device backup and restore behavior is controlled by iOS and your settings.

Local network communication

With your permission, the app connects to the configured controller to send commands and read its status, schedules, and weather information. This controller protocol is not encrypted or authenticated. Use it only on a trusted local network and do not expose its port to the internet.

You can revoke Local Network permission in iOS Settings. Demo mode uses simulated data and does not connect to a controller. The app does not request your iPhone's location to display controller weather.

Other services

The controller's own firmware and any services it uses, such as a weather provider, operate separately from the iPhone app. Their data handling depends on your controller configuration and those services' policies.

Apple may handle App Store, TestFlight, diagnostic, and feedback information under its own policies and your settings. This policy does not replace Apple's privacy policy.

This website and support

This site uses no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies set by this site's code, or third-party fonts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information such as IP addresses under GitHub's privacy statement.

If you contact support through GitHub Issues, your submission is public and handled by GitHub. Do not include private information. Support submissions are not automatically collected by the app.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions, open a privacy support request without including sensitive details. A GitHub account is required.

This policy will be updated if the app's data handling changes. The latest revision will be available on this page.
